## What happened

addresses**. My Step-0 filing did that correctly: every span carried `findings/01-spark-steering.md`
plus a section.

**stripped the per-span addresses too**, leaving only a "Reading access" list of three trees, one of
which was `DENSITY-CHAIN.md`. The grounding seat did exactly what its definition told it to do: it
checked the locator it was offered, found the spans were not in that file, and charged
`locator_unresolvable`.

It was right. Its own words: *"Per G-D1 the seat checks the locator that was offered, not the one
that would have worked."*

The seat then located the real addresses anyway and reported them — Span A at
`01-spark-steering.md:150-153`, Span B at `:155-159`, Span C at `:76-78`, Span D at `:37-38` — which
is how I know the spans themselves were faithful.
